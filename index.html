Our Story

A little corner of calm

☕

Pinary's Home Cafe is a small passion project born out of a love for carefully made drinks and homemade sweets. Based in Sydney, everything is prepared in small batches — never rushed, never mass-produced.

From our matcha lattes and cold brews to our homemade Basque cheesecake and Butterteok, each item is made with intention and care. We believe the best things come from someone's kitchen — and we want you to taste that difference.

Whether you're a regular or ordering for the first time, we hope every bite and sip feels like a little moment for yourself.
